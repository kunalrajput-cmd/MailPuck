MailPuck is a local macOS menu-bar utility that works with your own Apple Mail installation to read messages, generate draft replies using an AI model, and place those drafts back into Apple Mail. MailPuck never sends email on your behalf. Every draft waits for you to review and send it yourself.

MailPuck currently runs on a "bring your own key" model: the app talks directly to your chosen AI provider from your own Mac, with no MailPuck server in between reading or routing your email content.

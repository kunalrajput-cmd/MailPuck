Three ideas govern everything MailPuck does with your inbox.

Prepared, not automatic

The work begins before your review, never before it. Nothing leaves your computer without deliberate approval.

Considered, not generic

Each draft reflects context, relationship, and purpose, avoiding the hollow phrasing common to standard AI writing tools.

Controlled, not careless

Built for professionals who handle sensitive correspondence. If a message needs no response, MailPuck stays silent.

Built for review,
not blind delegation.

MailPuck helps you review communication with intention. It does not send on your behalf. The final decision stays visible, editable, and yours.

Security and integrity.

Four commitments, stated plainly rather than badged.

Draft-only architecture

MailPuck writes standard native drafts inside Apple's native mailing app. It has no code, interface, or permission to send email.

Zero autonomous outbound communication

Direct provider key (BYOK)

Draft requests go directly to your personal AI provider account using your key. Your data stays governed by your direct provider agreement.

Direct client to API architecture

Local desktop execution

MailPuck runs as a lightweight Mac menu bar app. No intermediary cloud server stores or indexes your mailbox threads.

Local device data containment

Transparent product roadmap

Requirements for MailPuck Mac.

Built for speed, direct local execution, and total data containment. Stated plainly.

Apple hardware

  • Supported computers: MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio, and Mac Pro.
  • Operating system: macOS Sonoma, Ventura, or Sequoia.
  • Desktop native: runs quietly as a menu bar tool with near-zero battery draw.

Client integration

  • Client requirement: uses Apple's native mailing app built into your Mac.
  • All office email apps: full support for Google Workspace, Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook, Exchange, and iCloud.
  • Draft-only safety: MailPuck never sends an email. Drafts wait in your drafts folder for review.

BYOK architecture

  • Bring your own key: connect your personal Gemini API key. Google's free tier keeps draft costs near zero.
  • Coming soon: Windows, iPhone and iPad companions, and browser extensions.

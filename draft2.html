Philosophy

Make room for work that matters.

Our mission is to remove repetitive composition from the center of the working day. Your attention belongs on the work that depends on your talent, judgement, and imagination.

For a finance professional, that is the decision behind the figures. For an academic, it is research and teaching that cannot be reduced to a template. For a leader, it is the direction only you can set.

From an hour of email to fifteen minutes of review.

We believe email should require deliberate review, not hours of blank page drafting. MailPuck prepares the drafts quietly in the background so you can focus on judgement: editing, approving, or deciding not to send.

Product Principles

Arrive at the inbox with the first thought already made.

When a message genuinely requires your response, a draft is ready for you to consider. You refine it, alter it, discard it, or write something new. MailPuck never replaces judgement. It gives that moment a better starting point.

PRINCIPLE 01

Prepared, not automatic

The work begins before the review, never before your human decision. Nothing leaves your computer without your deliberate approval.

PRINCIPLE 02

Considered, not generic

The draft reflects context, relationship, and purpose. It eliminates the hollow phrasing common in standard artificial intelligence tools.

PRINCIPLE 03

Controlled, not careless

Designed for professionals who handle sensitive communications. If a message needs no response, MailPuck remains silent.

Current Scope

System & Mail Client

Hardware and Software

  • Requires a Mac: Runs natively on MacBook, iMac, and Mac desktop computers running a recent version of macOS.
  • Uses Apple's native mail app: Integrates directly with the Mail app built into your Mac.
  • Works with all office email providers: If your account connects to Apple's native mail app (Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft Outlook, Microsoft 365, Exchange, or iCloud), MailPuck drafts for it.
Setup & Intelligence

Model Key & Platforms

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Connect your personal Gemini API key. Google provides a free usage tier, keeping draft costs at zero or pennies.
  • Draft only operation: MailPuck never sends mail autonomously. Every draft waits safely in your drafts folder.
  • Not yet available on other platforms: Windows, iPhone, iPad, and web mail versions are not supported today. They are planned for future versions.

Draft Only Architecture

MailPuck writes standard native drafts inside Apple's native mail app. It has no code, interface mechanism, or permission to send email.

Zero autonomous outbound communication

Direct Provider Key (BYOK)

Draft requests go directly to your personal AI provider account using your key. Your data remains governed by your direct provider agreement.

System Scope & Readiness

Requirements for MailPuck Mac.

MailPuck Mac is built for speed, direct local execution, and total data containment. We state our current operating requirements plainly.

Current Release · Mac Only

Apple Hardware

  • Supported computers: MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio, and Mac Pro laptops and desktops.
  • Operating system: macOS Sonoma, macOS Ventura, or macOS Sequoia.
  • Desktop native: Runs quietly as a discreet menu bar tool with near-zero battery draw.
Mailing Client & Inboxes

Client Integration

  • Client requirement: Uses Apple's native mailing app built into your Mac.
  • All office email apps: If your inbox connects to Apple's native mailing app, MailPuck Mac works with it. Full support for Google Workspace, Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook, Exchange, and iCloud.
  • Draft only safety: MailPuck Mac never sends an email. Drafts wait safely in your drafts folder for review.
Intelligence & Future Platforms

BYOK Architecture

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Connect your personal Gemini API key. Google provides a generous free tier for personal keys, keeping draft costs at zero.
  • Coming soon: Windows desktop edition, iPhone and iPad companion apps, and native browser extensions are planned for future versions.
Philosophy

Make room for work that matters.

Our mission is to remove repetitive composition from the center of the working day. Your attention belongs on the work that depends on your talent, judgement, and imagination.

For a finance professional, that is the decision behind the numbers. For an academic, it is research and teaching that cannot be reduced to a template. For a leader, it is the direction only you can provide.

From an hour of email to fifteen minutes of review.

We believe email should require deliberate review, not hours of blank page drafting. MailPuck Mac prepares drafts quietly in the background so you can focus on judgement: editing, approving, or deciding not to send.

BEFORE 60 minutes of reading, context switching, and blank page composition
AFTER 15 minutes of reviewing, refining, and deciding

Product Principles

Arrive at the inbox with the first thought already made.

When a message genuinely requires your response, a draft is ready for you to consider. You refine it, alter it, discard it, or write something new. MailPuck Mac gives that moment a better starting point.

PRINCIPLE 01

Prepared, not automatic

The work begins before the review, never before your human decision. Nothing leaves your computer without your deliberate approval.

PRINCIPLE 02

Considered, not generic

The draft reflects context, relationship, and purpose. It eliminates the hollow phrasing common in standard artificial intelligence tools.

PRINCIPLE 03

Controlled, not careless

Designed for professionals who handle sensitive communications. If a message needs no response, MailPuck Mac remains silent.

Security and Integrity

Built for review, not blind delegation.

MailPuck Mac helps you review communications with intention. It does not send on your behalf. The final decision remains visible, editable, and yours.

Draft Only Architecture

MailPuck Mac writes standard native drafts inside Apple's native mailing app. It has no code, interface mechanism, or permission to send email.

Zero autonomous outbound communication

Direct Provider Key (BYOK)

Draft requests go directly to your personal AI provider account using your key. Your data remains governed by your direct provider agreement.

Direct client to API architecture

Local Desktop Execution

MailPuck Mac runs as a lightweight Mac menu bar app. No intermediary cloud server stores or indexes your mailbox threads.

Local device data containment
